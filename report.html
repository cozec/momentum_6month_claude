Strategy A · Nasdaq-100 · 6-month momentum, monthly rebalance

L=6m / P=1m · baseline / project default

How this strategy works

On the first trading day of each month, rank every point-in-time Nasdaq-100 member by 6-month momentum — the average of its last six monthly returns, excluding the current month to avoid look-ahead. Buy the top 3, equal-weighted (⅓ each), and hold for one month; then re-rank and rotate. The universe uses historical (point-in-time) index membership to avoid survivorship bias, and results are benchmarked against buy-and-hold QQQ and 3× TQQQ.

Strategy B · S&P 500 (ex Nasdaq-100 picks) · 6-month momentum, monthly rebalance

L=6m / P=1m · same rules, broader universe, complementary basket

How this strategy works

The exact same rules as Strategy A — rank by 6-month momentum, buy the top 3 equal-weighted, hold one month, rotate — but over the S&P 500 universe. Because the Nasdaq-100 sits inside the S&P 500, each month this basket excludes the tickers Strategy A already holds, so the two baskets never overlap and together give a diversified 6-stock view. Same QQQ / 3× TQQQ benchmarks. (S&P universe uses the current constituent snapshot.)
