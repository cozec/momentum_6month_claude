## How to read the Tag column (decision aid, not the strategy)

The strategy only ranks by Mom/mo and buys the top 3. Tags blend the score with trend
health, using a fixed rule on Δ 6-mo high (DD) and Last mo (1M):

| Tag | Rule | Names |
|-----|------|-------|
| **Buy** | DD within 10% and 1M ≥ 0 | AMD, CRWD, DDOG, PANW, FTNT, WDAY, CSCO |
| **Hold** | DD within 20% (and not Buy) | MU, MRVL, TXN |
| **Watch** | DD 20–30% and 1M > −10% | INTC, STX, AMAT, LRCX, QCOM, MSTR |
| **Caution** | DD worse than 30%, or 20%+ with 1M ≤ −10% | SNDK, ARM, WDC, KLAC |

**Theme:** the entire top 20 is tech — overwhelmingly **semiconductors + storage** (memory/HBM/NAND/HDD/semi-cap) plus **cybersecurity** (CRWD, PANW, FTNT in the top 11) and **AI software**. That concentration is itself a risk: these names rise and fall together, so a basket from this list is effectively one macro bet on the AI/semis cycle.
