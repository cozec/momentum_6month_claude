How this strategy works

On the first trading day of each month, rank every point-in-time Nasdaq-100 member by 6-month momentum — the average of its last six monthly returns, excluding the current month to avoid look-ahead. Buy the top 3, equal-weighted (⅓ each), and hold for one month; then re-rank and rotate. The universe uses historical (point-in-time) index membership to avoid survivorship bias, and results are benchmarked against buy-and-hold QQQ and 3× TQQQ.

Strategy B · S&P 500 (excluding Strategy A’s holdings) · 6-month momentum, monthly rebalance

L=6m / P=1m · same rules, point-in-time S&P 500 universe, benchmarked vs SPY

How this strategy works

The exact same rules as Strategy A — rank by 6-month momentum, buy the top 3 equal-weighted, hold one month, rotate — but over the S&P 500, using point-in-time membership (a stock is eligible only while it was in the index). Each month it skips the stocks Strategy A holds, so the two baskets never hold the same name — but they are not diversified: both lean heavily on tech, and their monthly returns are clearly correlated (≈0.5). Benchmarked against buy-and-hold SPY. Former members that Yahoo no longer prices are missing, so some survivorship bias remains.
